<objective>
Fix the SLO freshness validator to emit `pass` status for scrapers within the 24h SLO window, unblocking the nightly deploy pipeline.

Purpose: The deploy gate (`check_slo_gate()`) queries `slo_logs` for `status='pass'` rows. Currently `check_freshness()` only emits `skip` or `fail` -- never `pass`. This means the nightly deploy always aborts even when all scrapers are healthy. This single-plan fix corrects the status logic and adds test coverage.

Output: Fixed `slo_validator.py`, new `test_slo_validator.py`, updated `test_deploy_validator.py`.
</objective>
